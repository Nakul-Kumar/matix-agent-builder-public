# Code Of Conduct

Matix Agent Builder welcomes constructive contributors. Keep discussion
focused, respectful, and useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Critique code, designs, and decisions without attacking people.
- Share enough context for others to understand your feedback.
- Respect privacy. Do not post secrets, private data, or personal information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Deliberately disrupting issues, pull requests, discussions, or the service.
- Submitting malware, credential theft, or abusive automation.

## Reporting

For conduct or safety concerns, use GitHub's private reporting tools when
available, or open a public issue only when it does not require sensitive
details. Maintainers may remove content, close threads, block accounts, or take
other reasonable moderation actions.
