SCOPE: Two small calibration tweaks on the existing light theme — no structural changes, no token renaming, no component refactors. Just nudge two sets of values.

TWEAK 1 — DECORATION BLOBS: lift visibility slightly

The current --decor-* blobs blend too far into the cream background. Bump their opacity and saturation by a small, controlled amount so they read as intentional shapes, not residual paper texture.

Update the decor opacity range from 0.18–0.28 to 0.30–0.40. Apply at the higher end (0.36–0.40) for the larger background blobs and the lower end (0.30–0.32) for the smaller accent blobs.

Also deepen each decor token by ~one shade so the saturation reads better at the new opacity:

   --decor-1: #9E8E70   /* was #A89A7A — slightly deeper warm taupe */
   --decor-2: #B89684   /* was #C5A89A — slightly deeper dusty clay */
   --decor-3: #7F9474   /* was #8FA084 — slightly deeper sage */
   --decor-4: #C5A580   /* was #D4B89E — slightly deeper warm sand */
   --decor-5: #8A7565   /* was #9C8472 — slightly deeper walnut */

Do NOT push past these values. The blobs should be clearly visible as shapes but still sit BEHIND the content — they must not become a competing layer. If after the change any blob reads as "in the foreground" or distracts from text, pull that one back to opacity 0.28.

Keep the existing mask-image central-clearance — the center of the page stays clean. Only the edges get the slight lift.

TWEAK 2 — TEXT: deepen the bright/muted tokens a small amount

Body and muted text are reading a touch too light against cream, which is hurting legibility. Deepen these tokens by a controlled amount — not a full plunge to black, just enough to lift contrast:

   --text-primary:  #1F1810   /* was #2A2014 — slightly deeper warm near-black */
   --text-body:     #312818   /* was #3D3424 — slightly deeper warm brown */
   --text-muted:    #5E5240   /* was #7A6F5A — clearly muted but readable */
   --text-faint:    #877B62   /* was #9D9277 — for placeholder/disabled only */

That's it for text — do not touch headings' weight, size, font, or any layout property. The change is color-only on these four tokens.
