The prompt-card scroll container works but doesn't visually announce itself as a contained scrollable region. The mask fade at top and bottom is too subtle to register as a boundary. Add a custom hairline scroll-position rail on the right edge of the container — visible at rest, terracotta thumb that tracks scroll progress. Subtle, editorial, not a chunky native scrollbar.

Implementation:

1. The scroll container keeps its existing properties: height 440px, overflow-y scroll, hidden native scrollbar, mask fade. Do not change those.

2. Place a custom rail element absolutely positioned along the right edge of the scroll container. Specifically:
   - Position: absolute, top: 8px, bottom: 8px, right: 2px, width: 1px.
   - Background color: #2A2420 (hairline color).
   - The rail's parent must be position: relative — set that on the scroll container's IMMEDIATE wrapper, not the scroll container itself (because position: relative on the scroll container would shift internal layout).
   - The rail is purely visual and pointer-events: none.

3. Inside the rail, render a thumb element absolutely positioned:
   - Width: 1px (same as the rail, so it covers it).
   - Background color: terracotta primary #C2674A.
   - Height: dynamic — set to (visibleHeight / contentHeight) * railHeight, clamped to a minimum of 32px so it never becomes a tiny dot. With visible 440px and content ~510px, the thumb will be roughly 86% of the rail height.
   - Top position: dynamic — set to (scrollTop / (contentHeight - visibleHeight)) * (railHeight - thumbHeight). Updates on scroll.
   - Use a scroll event listener on the scroll container to update the thumb's top position in real time. Throttle with requestAnimationFrame.
   - Transition: top 60ms linear (so motion feels responsive, not laggy).

4. Add two tiny bracket marks at the top-left and bottom-right corners of the scroll container as a printerly affordance — these signal "framed region" without a full border:
   - Top-left bracket: a 10px wide x 10px tall L-shape (two 1px lines, one horizontal one vertical), color #2A2420, positioned at top-left of the scroll container, offset -4px on both axes so the brackets sit just outside the card edge.
   - Bottom-right bracket: the mirror L-shape at bottom-right of the scroll container, offset -4px on both axes.
   - Implement as two pseudo-elements (::before for top-left, ::after for bottom-right) on the scroll container's wrapper, OR as two small absolutely positioned <span> elements with two 1px borders each.

5. The existing "—— SCROLL FOR MORE" hint can stay as-is. The thumb + brackets are additional signal, not replacement.

6. Verification:
   - At rest the rail is visible as a faint vertical hairline on the right edge of the scroll container.
   - The terracotta thumb occupies roughly 86% of the rail's height (because most content is visible).
   - When you scroll inside the container, the thumb visibly moves downward.
   - Two small L-shaped brackets are visible at the top-left and bottom-right corners of the scroll container.
   - No other visual changes anywhere else on the page.
   - On viewports below 1024px (where the cards switch to horizontal-scroll row mode), the rail and brackets are hidden.

Do not change card content, card styling, mask fade, hover behavior, click behavior, the SCROLL FOR MORE hint, or anything in the left column.
